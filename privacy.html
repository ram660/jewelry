EFJ Studio privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

EFJ Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of Elegant Fashion Jewellery (Vancouver, Canada). It runs on the owner's own computer and publishes our own images (jewellery care and styling content) to our own social media accounts.

What it accesses

Only the accounts that belong to Elegant Fashion Jewellery. On Pinterest, it reads the names of our own boards and creates Pins on them. It does not read, collect or store any other person's Pins, boards, profile or personal information.

What it stores

An access token for our own account, kept on the owner's computer and never shared. A record of the Pins we published (title, link and date). No visitor or customer data is collected by this tool.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. No one other than the owner uses the tool.

This website

This site lists our product picks. Some links are affiliate links: as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. The site sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics; the stores you visit through a link have their own privacy policies.

Contact and removal

Questions, or a request to remove anything we published: message us on Instagram at @elegant_jewellss.
